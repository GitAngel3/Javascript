Sales track

You are helping a small online store keep track of its sales. The store sold 5 t-shirts, each for ₹400, and 3 caps, each for ₹150. Your task is to: Create variables to store the number of items sold and their prices. Calculate the total earnings from the sales. Display the total earnings. Change the price of the t-shirts to ₹450 and recalculate the total earnings. Print the updated total.

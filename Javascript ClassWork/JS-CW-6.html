A simple product listing feature for an online grocery store

You have an array of product names and an array of their prices. Your tasks are: Merge the product names and prices into one array in the format: "Product: Apple - Price: 50" Display all product entries using a loop.Filter and show only the products with prices greater than 50. Add a new product "Watermelon" with price 80 to the list, and then display the updated product list.
